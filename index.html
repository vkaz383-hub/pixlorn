Privacy Policy for Pixlorn HyperClean Pro

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixlorn HyperClean Pro ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding your data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Pixlorn HyperClean Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).

  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical locations.
  • We do not access, view, or upload photos, documents, contacts, or personal user files from your device.
  • All diagnostic metrics (cleaning counters, audio wave generations, CPU load estimates) are executed strictly offline and locally on your device.

2. Device Hardware Permissions

The application requests minimal permissions solely necessary for device functionality:

  • Audio Output / Speaker Access: Used exclusively to play the low-frequency resonant sound waves (165 Hz, 240 Hz, 80 Hz) for speaker moisture and dust ejection.
  • Vibration (Haptics): Synchronized with speaker frequencies to provide mechanical assistance in water droplet removal and UI tactile feedback.
  • Internet Access: Required solely for loading advertisements served by Google AdMob.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our application utilizes Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous data in accordance with its privacy terms, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Device model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
  • Ad interaction performance metrics (impressions, clicks).

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:
Google Ads & Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

Pixlorn HyperClean Pro does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose utility designed for device maintenance.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
